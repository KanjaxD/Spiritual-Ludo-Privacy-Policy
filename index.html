1. About this policy

This policy explains information used by Spiritual Ludo (the "game" or "application") and its third-party advertising service provider, Google AdMob.

The game features no account registration, developer-operated cloud save service, social network login, or developer-operated tracking analytics. The term "local storage" describes the few preferences saved on your device; it does not mean advertising services collect no technical network data.

2. Game data stored on your device

Spiritual Ludo keeps the current game in device memory while the app is open. It does not restore board progress after the app closes. The current session includes:

  • Active board positions, token coordinates, and turn progression for Spiritual Ludo;
  • Current square positions and turns for the 100-Square Snake & Ladder journey;
  • Current-session sound and other game settings.

Local storage retains only the board-column layout preference and first-launch privacy-notice acknowledgment between sessions. Game data is not uploaded to a developer server. Ad frequency counters and transition animation states are also held only in temporary memory for the current session.

An older version may have stored a shop profile locally; this version no longer uses it.

The Android application is configured to exclude app data from automatic Android cloud backups and device-transfer backups. Operating systems and device manufacturers may manage system-level backups outside the application's direct control. Browser local storage is separate from Android app storage.

3. Advertising data and Google AdMob

The Android application integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • Rewarded Video Ads (Optional): Rewarded ads are entirely optional and only requested when you voluntarily tap the in-game "Grace (6)" or blessing button to summon a divine roll of 6 or obtain spiritual rerolls.
  • Interstitial Ads (Frequency-Capped): The application may display a non-rewarded full-screen interstitial ad at natural break points, such as completing a game (e.g. reaching the Vaikuntha sanctum in Ludo or attaining GOD at Square 100 in Snake & Ladder). Interstitials are session-delayed, frequency-capped, and are never intentionally displayed during active pawn movement, active dice rolling, on app launch, or after every single turn.
  • Offline Playability: The game remains fully playable if network connectivity is disabled or if advertising requests fail. A failed ad request never blocks or penalizes your gameplay action.

Google and its advertising service providers may automatically collect or receive technical device data, including:

  • IP addresses (used to infer approximate general location, such as city or country);
  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID / AAID and App-Set ID);
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, including app launches, taps, impressions, and video views;
  • Diagnostic, performance, and crash metrics.

This data is processed by Google for ad delivery, impression measurement, fraud prevention, and security. Spiritual Ludo applies a conservative general-audience content rating limit and under-age-of-consent treatment; Google's consent choices determine which ad serving modes are permitted. The developer does not receive a copy of your game session through AdMob, but may view aggregated analytical reports provided by Google. No independent tracking SDKs, crash analytics, or third-party mediation networks have been added by the developer.

Google processes information under its own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Ad Settings & Personalization Controls: https://myadcenter.google.com/

4. Privacy choices and consent

The first-launch privacy notice is presented once upon initial installation. Continuing acknowledges this policy; it is not permission for personalized advertising. You can review this full policy from within the game at any time (Main Menu > Privacy).

Where required by applicable regional laws (such as the European Economic Area / GDPR, UK, and relevant state regulations), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides explicit choice screens prior to ad requests. If consent checks fail or ad serving is disallowed, ad requests are prevented.

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, the game shows Settings > Google Ad Choices so you can update your advertising choices. Depending on your Android version, you may also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your device system settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

You can always choose not to watch a rewarded video ad. An interrupted or prematurely closed ad does not grant in-game Grace unless Google confirms completion. Interstitial ads do not grant virtual rewards and can be closed using the dismiss controls (X) supplied by the ad provider.

5. Retention and deletion

Current game progress ends when the app closes. Settings > Reset Progress restarts the current board, but leaves the saved column preference and first-launch acknowledgment. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes those local preferences and any shop-profile data left by an older version.

To permanently erase all local application data, navigate to:
Android Settings > Apps > Spiritual Ludo > Storage > Clear Storage (or uninstall the application). Browser users can clear site storage directly within their browser settings.

Because the developer does not maintain remote user accounts or databases, the developer cannot remotely retrieve, restore, or delete game progress. There is no remote account to delete. Clearing local app data does not delete information already processed by Google AdMob; Google's retention rules and deletion controls govern data held by Google.

Support messages or privacy inquiries voluntarily sent to the developer are used solely to resolve your request and fulfill legal obligations. Do not send sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

6. Device permissions and security

Spiritual Ludo requests only essential network permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load advertisements, verify connectivity, and retrieve Google UMP consent dialogues.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to monitor network availability.

The merged Android manifest includes advertising-ID permissions and standard foreground/wake-lock support required by Google Android libraries.

Explicit Permissions Not Requested: Spiritual Ludo does NOT request access to your camera, microphone, device contacts, call history, SMS messages, external photos/media files, precise GPS location, or biometric sensors. Game illustrations, dice animations, board textures, and sound effects are bundled locally within the application package.

The application disables cleartext HTTP traffic in its Android security configuration (enforcing encrypted HTTPS). Third-party advertising partners utilize their own industry-standard administrative and technical security measures.
